Top #1 achievement

Platform migration — OVH Nomad to multi-site Kubernetes on AWS

Roadmap-level move from Nomad on OVH to a US + EU footprint on Amazon EKS — modern control plane, cluster add-ons, and operations patterns aligned with current AWS best practice (security groups, IRSA, observability, GitOps-ready delivery).

  • From Nomad on OVH — consolidate orchestration on managed Kubernetes instead of self-run schedulers where it reduces toil and improves standard tooling.
  • Multi-region by design — separate clusters (or cell-style layouts) for United States and Europe to match data residency, latency, and DR expectations.
  • State-of-the-art baseline — hardened node groups, ingress and service mesh options as needed, secrets integration, autoscaling, and continuous alignment with AWS Well-Architected and your compliance targets (e.g. ISO / SOC operating model).
