Public timeline highlights

  • 2019: Public sources describe Jusmundi as founded in Paris and focused on arbitration intelligence.
  • 2022: Public company pages include Jus Connect as part of the product ecosystem timeline.

Public positioning and products

Public announcements describe Jus AI as a research and productivity assistant with citation-focused workflows.
